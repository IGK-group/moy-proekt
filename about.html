About

GlideHome — home cleaning you don't have to do by hand

We source and tune robots for the chores that aren't worth your time: window cleaning, floors. Fewer ladders, fewer rags, fewer weekends spent cleaning.

Why we do this

Windows get cleaned twice a year because it's awkward and unsafe: a ladder, rags, streaks, a balcony up high. The robot takes all of that on — it grips the glass, plans a route and covers the surface itself.

We started with a single model for apartment windows, added a version for panoramic glass and storefronts, then expanded the lineup to robot vacuums. Same idea throughout: machines do the repetitive work, people don't.

How we work

We pick platforms for how securely they hold and how predictable the route is — not for the number of modes in the ad. Every model is tested on real windows — apartment, panoramic, storefront — before it goes in the catalog.

Support

Warranty, 30-day returns and live support in English and Russian. Guides and videos are in the support center.
