GlideHome technology

What makes the clean reliable

Triple fall-safety

Stays on the glass even if the power drops

A vacuum turbine, a backup battery and a safety rope. Pull the plug and the robot holds the glass and lowers gently instead of falling.

Edge detection

Reaches the frame and doesn't miss the border

Perimeter sensors map the window's edges. The robot cleans right up to the frame — no leftover strip along the edge.

Smart path planning

Zig-zag pass plus a check lap around the perimeter

Multi-sensor route planning: full N-shaped coverage, then a finishing pass along the edges.

Quiet operation

Around 60 dB — run it during a call

Noise level is close to normal conversation. The robot works in the background and stays out of the way.
