FAQ

W17S questions

Will the robot fall off the window?+
No. Suction holds the body, there's a backup battery and a safety rope to the frame or handle.
Will it work on a two-panel window?+
Yes, but the robot cleans one plane per run — on a large split window you reposition it. For floor-to-ceiling glass the DYP is a better fit.
What's in the box?+
Robot, power supply with cable, safety rope, a set of microfiber pads, a bottle of concentrate, remote.
