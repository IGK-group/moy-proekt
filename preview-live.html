Good to know

Questions people ask before buying

Which window robot should I get?+

The W17S ($129.99) is for flat indoor glass — windows, mirrors, shower doors, glass tables. The DYP ($269.99) adds a UPS backup battery and a safety rope, so it's the one for tall, floor-to-ceiling or exterior windows.

Will it fall off the glass?+

Strong suction holds the robot on vertical glass, and edge sensors make it turn before the frame. The DYP goes further: a built-in UPS keeps suction alive through a power cut, and a safety rope is included.

What surfaces do the window robots work on?+

Flat glass: windows, mirrors, shower doors and glass tabletops. Minimum window size for the DYP is 35 × 35 cm.

How long does a window take?+

The DYP cleans at about 0.23 m²/min — a standard window is done in a few minutes. Press start and walk away.

Do the robot vacuums really mop?+

Yes. Both vacuums sweep, suction and mop in one automatic pass. The 3-in-1 has a 300 ml tank; the Ultra-Thin has an auto-refill reservoir that keeps the pad wet for the whole cycle.

Will the vacuum fit under my furniture?+

The Ultra-Thin model is built low on purpose — it slides under sofas, beds and cabinets where dust collects. It covers up to 150 m² on one 4,400 mAh charge.

How are they powered?+

Window robots run on mains power (the DYP is 100–240V and works worldwide). Robot vacuums charge over USB or a dock and run 60–120 minutes per charge.

Shipping, returns and warranty?+

Free shipping on every order, delivered in 5–11 business days. 30-day returns, no questions asked, plus a 2-year warranty on every GlideHome device.
