FAQ

Common questions

Will the robot fall off the window?+
No. The vacuum turbine holds the body to the glass, there's a backup battery in case the power is cut, and a safety rope that clips to the frame or handle.
What surfaces can I use it on?+
Smooth glass, mirrors, tile, polished stone. Matte and textured surfaces won't work.
Does it work without Wi-Fi?+
Yes. Basic modes start from the button on the body or the remote. Wi-Fi is only for the app and updates.
How long does one window take?+
A standard apartment window is about 3–5 minutes depending on dirt and mode.
Does it need a permanent power cable?+
Yes, it runs from mains power via a long cable. The built-in battery is a safety backup for power loss.
How loud is it?+
Around 60 dB — comparable to normal conversation.
How do I start a return?+
Message support with your order number within 30 days. We'll send a prepaid label and refund you once it arrives.
